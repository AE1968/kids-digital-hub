Goodnight Moon Bear

A soothing bedtime story regarding a little bear and the magical night sky.

Written & Illustrated by Kids Digital Hub (AI)

🌙

Barnaby Bear was very, very sleepy.
He rubbed his eyes and looked out of his window.

"The sun has gone to bed," he whispered.

🦉

Outside, the stars were beginning to twinkle.
Twinkle, twinkle, little light...

"Goodnight, Mr. Owl in the tree," said Barnaby softly.

💤

Barnaby climbed into his warm, cozy bed.
He pulled the fluffiest blanket up to his nose.

"Goodnight, Moon," he yawned.

Sweet Dreams!

🐻❤️

And just like that, Barnaby was fast asleep.
